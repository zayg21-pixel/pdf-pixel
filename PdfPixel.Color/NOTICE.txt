[ Compact ICC Profiles ]

The CMYK profile in 'Profiles/CompactCmyk.icc' is
'CGATS001Compat-v2-micro.icc' published at
https://github.com/saucecontrol/Compact-ICC-Profiles, with one tag added: a
'B2A0' table, generated with ArgyllCMS by inverting the profile's own 'A2B0'
table. The original tags are unchanged. It is embedded into
'PdfPixel.Color.dll'.
